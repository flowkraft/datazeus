## What goes wrong

{/* BEAT 4 — the failure this prevents, and how you would notice it in real data.
    Every episode in this track earns its place by preventing a wrong number or a
    job that dies at 3 a.m. Name that here.

    THE COLD OPEN, and it is a real bug in our own product: bkend-boot-groovy-playground's
    BkendApplication is a bare @SpringBootApplication with no @EnableScheduling anywhere in the
    tree. Spring Boot does not turn scheduling on for you — its autoconfigured task scheduler is
    itself conditional on the bean @EnableScheduling registers. So the documented ritual (copy
    crons.groovy, uncomment @Scheduled, rebuild) yields an app that starts clean, logs nothing,
    and never runs your transform. No error, no stack trace, stale dashboards.

    THEN: a schedule whose expression is compiled into the jar, so changing 2 a.m. to 3 a.m.
    means a rebuild. And two instances of the app running the same nightly load twice. */}
