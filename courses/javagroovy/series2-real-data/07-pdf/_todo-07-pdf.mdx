    WRITING — five, and every one ships a document that opens fine:
      · a NULL renders as the word "null". Series 1 · 30, somewhere it cannot be shrugged off.
      · formatting scattered through the template, so one column disagrees with the next.
        Format once, in one place.
      · FONTS ARE WHERE ENCODING COMES BACK. PDFBox's built-ins are WinAnsi, so Ștefan and
        Müller throw or vanish. Embed a font, or mangle people's names — Series 1 · 20 with a
        customer's name on the line.
      · a subtotal computed per RENDERED PAGE instead of per group. Totals come from the data,

      · a wrong baseUri: relative CSS, images and fonts silently fail to resolve, and you get
        an unstyled PDF with no error at all.

    that is right, and a scanned page yields zero rows without anything throwing.

    produced it, or the data against a stated subtotal printed on the page itself — or you are
