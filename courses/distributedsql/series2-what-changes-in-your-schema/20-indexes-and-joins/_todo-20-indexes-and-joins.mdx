                 Here it is its own set of ranges with its own replica groups, so an insert
                 touching three indexes coordinates with three groups — a multiple, not a
                 percentage. Hence: fewer, wider indexes; covering columns so a read is one
                 lookup rather than a hop to another machine; and real willingness to delete
                 indexes nobody uses. Learn SQL, Series 3 · 25 said an index has a cost; this
                 is that sentence with a far larger number in it.
       JOINS     the question is WHICH SIDE MOVES. A small table can be sent everywhere; a
                 large one cannot, so both sides redistribute by the join key. Co-locating
                 tables that are always joined turns a distributed join into a local one, and
                 that is a design-time decision rather than a tuning one.
