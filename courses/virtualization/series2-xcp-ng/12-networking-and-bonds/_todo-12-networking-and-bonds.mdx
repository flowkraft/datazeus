     COVER: networking, in this platform's nouns, and the design decisions that are hard to
     change later.
       PIF   a physical interface on a host. VIF a virtual one on a VM. A NETWORK joins them
             across the pool, which is why a network must exist identically on every host or
             a VM cannot migrate to that host.
       BONDS for redundancy and throughput, and the modes, briefly.
       VLANS as the normal way a pool serves several networks over one pair of cables.
       THE MANAGEMENT INTERFACE, which is what the pool uses to talk to itself, and which you
             do not casually reconfigure remotely.
     THE DESIGN RULE FOR A DATA WORKLOAD, and it lands on Storage for Data, Series 4 · 10's
     argument: SEPARATE THE STORAGE NETWORK FROM THE VM NETWORK. If your VMs' disks are on
     NFS or iSCSI, then every read your database does crosses a cable, and putting a nightly
     extract on the same cable at the same time is how you produce a mystery.
     THE WORKPLACE MOMENT: "the database got slow every night at two" is a storage network
     sharing a link with a backup job, more often than it is anything in the database.
