                                  the world through the host. Nothing on the LAN can reach
                                  the guest. Fine for a laptop, wrong for a server.
       A REAL BRIDGE              the guest gets an address on YOUR network from YOUR DHCP,
                                  and is a peer of every other machine. This is what you want
                                  on a host that other people connect to, and it is the
