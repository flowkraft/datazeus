     COVER: three different mechanisms people call "redundancy", and what each survives.
       RAID              mirroring or parity across disks in one machine. Survives a disk.
       REPLICATION       whole copies on other machines. Survives a machine, a rack, a site.
       ERASURE CODING    data split into k data and m parity shards, any k of which rebuild
                         the object. Survives m failures at a fraction of replication's
                         space — 1.5x rather than 3x is typical — and costs CPU and network
                         on every read that has to reconstruct.
