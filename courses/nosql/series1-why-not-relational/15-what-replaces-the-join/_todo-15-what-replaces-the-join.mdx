                      it grows without limit if you are not careful.
       REFERENCE      store an id and fetch separately. Small, and it is N+1 by hand.
       DUPLICATE THE FIELDS YOU DISPLAY  the extended-reference idea: copy the three
                      attributes the screen shows, accept that they can go stale, and decide
                      deliberately how stale is acceptable.
       JOIN IN THE APPLICATION  honest and common, and it moves the cost to a place with no
                      statistics and no optimizer.
       PRECOMPUTE     a second collection or table shaped like the answer, maintained on
                      write. The same idea as a materialized view, without the engine's help.
     THE RULE: the choice is decided by whether the embedded thing is BOUNDED and whether it
