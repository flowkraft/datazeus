## What goes wrong

{/* BEAT 4 — the wrong number this prevents, and how you would notice it.

    THE ACADEMY'S SELECTION RULE, EXACTLY — BOTH WAYS OF GETTING IT WRONG ARE SILENT:
      CLOSE TOO EARLY  real events arrive after the window shut and are dropped. The total is
                       wrong, and nothing raises.
      CLOSE TOO LATE   the number is never final, so a dashboard that must be right by 09:00
                       is still moving at 11:00.
    Neither is an error. That is the whole reason this episode exists.

    AND THE ONE THAT IS WORST BECAUSE IT LOOKS RIGHT: a window closed on PROCESSING time gives
    a Monday total that quietly includes Sunday's late arrivals and excludes its own. The
    report is wrong at both ends, and wrong differently every day depending on when the data
    happened to show up. */}
