    ── THE SENTENCE THE EPISODE IS BUILT ON ────────────────────────────────
    A REVERSE PROXY IS ONE PROCESS THAT LISTENS ON 80 AND 443 AND HANDS EACH REQUEST TO
    WHICHEVER LOCAL SERVICE SHOULD ANSWER IT — chosen by hostname, or by path. Everything
    else in this episode is a consequence of that one sentence.

    ── WHY A DATA PERSON NEEDS ONE, and the answer is already on their box ──
    By this point in the series they have code-server from 39, probably a MinIO console, a
    notebook, a scheduler UI, a dashboard. EVERY ONE OF THEM BINDS TO ITS OWN PORT ON
    localhost. Without a proxy the choice is to expose five ports to the internet — five
    things to secure, five certificates, five firewall rules — or to publish nothing. With
    one you get ONE DOOR, ONE CERTIFICATE, ONE PLACE TO PUT A PASSWORD.
