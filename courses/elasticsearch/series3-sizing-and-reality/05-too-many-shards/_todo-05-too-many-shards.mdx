     COVER: the failure this product is famous for and which nobody warns beginners about —
     TOO MANY SHARDS. Every shard costs heap on the master and the data nodes whether or not
     anybody queries it, so a year of daily indices with five shards each is nearly two
     thousand shards holding a few megabytes apiece, and the cluster becomes unstable for
     reasons that look like nothing.
     THE SYMPTOMS: slow cluster state updates, master instability, heap pressure with no
     query load. THE FIXES: fewer shards per index, rollover by size rather than by day, and
     shrink or delete old indices.
     THE RULE: shard count follows from DATA VOLUME, never from how many nodes you have or
     how important the index feels.
