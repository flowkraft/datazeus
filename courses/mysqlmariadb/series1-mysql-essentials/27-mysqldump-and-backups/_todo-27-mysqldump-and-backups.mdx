     COVER: mysqldump, and the two flags that decide whether the dump is worth anything.
       --single-transaction  a consistent InnoDB dump WITHOUT locking the tables. Omit it on
                             a live server and you either lock writers out or get a dump
                             that is internally inconsistent across tables.

     cleanly, looks complete, and is missing every stored procedure. Nobody finds out until
