    NEW 2026-09-19 — gaps and islands had no home in the course.
    GOAL: find runs of consecutive periods — when a streak started, when it stopped, how long it lasted — and
    the gaps between them.
    SQL, TWO WAYS, PROVEN EQUIVALENT:
      1. ROW_NUMBER over all the periods minus ROW_NUMBER over the flagged ones is constant within a run,
         so GROUP BY that difference gives one row per island.
      2. LAG marks where a run starts (1 when the previous period was not flagged), and a running SUM of
         the marks numbers the islands.
    The first is shorter; the second is the one people can read back a month later. Teach both.

    THE OPENER, A ONE-LINE RECAP OF SERIES 2 · 10: its weekly finding ("it started and it stopped"), now

      2024-10-28                   1 week       1 order     the lapse
    Then the lesson's own examples: a customer's longest run of ordering months; gaps in "OrderID".
