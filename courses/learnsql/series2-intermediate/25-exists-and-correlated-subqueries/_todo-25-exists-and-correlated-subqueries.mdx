    "Which employees manage nobody?"
      WHERE "EmployeeID" NOT IN (SELECT "ReportsTo" FROM "Employees")    -> NO ROWS AT ALL

    A CANDIDATE, RUN ON THE SHIPPED NORTHWIND (2026-09-05) — the orders whose lines add
    up to more than 50 units. Six come back:

      SELECT o."OrderID", o."ShipCountry"
      FROM "Orders" o
      WHERE EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM "Order Details" od
                    WHERE od."OrderID" = o."OrderID"
                    HAVING sum(od."Quantity") > 50)
      ORDER BY o."OrderID";
      -> 23 UK · 25 Germany · 43 Austria · 45 Germany · 63 Germany · +1

    The shape is the whole point: no GROUP BY anywhere, and that aggregate condition
    could not have gone in the subquery's WHERE — episode 35 spends its running time
    proving exactly why. CHECK THE THRESHOLD if the dataset ever changes: > 100 returns
    nothing on this one, and an example that returns no rows teaches the wrong lesson.
