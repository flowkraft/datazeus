<Beat title="Half-open ranges" />

{/* keep:half-open:0 */}
<Point title="SQL technique" side="left">

A half-open range: `>=` the first day `AND <` the first day of the next period. Back-to-back periods never share a day, and no 30th, 31st or 23:59:59 is needed.

</Point>
{/* /keep */}

<Say who="mnemosyne" pose="proud">

**The fix is a habit, not a better end date.** On or after the first day — **and strictly before the first day of the next month.**

</Say>

```sql {4-5}#good
SELECT count(*) AS "Shipments",
       max("ShippedDate") AS "Last shipped"
FROM "Orders"
WHERE "ShippedDate" >= DATE '2024-11-01'
  AND "ShippedDate" <  DATE '2024-12-01';
```

<Point title="SQL technique" side="left">

       count(o."OrderID") AS "Orders"

       TIMESTAMP '2024-12-01',
       INTERVAL '1 month') AS s(month)
LEFT JOIN "Orders" o

ORDER BY s.month;
