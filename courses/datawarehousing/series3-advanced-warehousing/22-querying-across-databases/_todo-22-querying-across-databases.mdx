      WHERE DOES THE WORK HAPPEN     the whole episode in one question. If the remote side applies your
                                     filter and your aggregate, a small answer comes back. If it cannot,
                                     the engine drags the whole table across the network and joins it
                                     locally — one second against 100,000 rows becomes twenty minutes
                                     against fifty million. PUSHDOWN is the word. Check it; never assume
                                     it.
      STATISTICS                     the local planner knows nothing about the remote table's size or
                                     distribution, so it guesses — and a join order chosen from a guess is
                                     where federated queries go wrong in ways the plan does not explain.
      THE BLAST RADIUS               a federated query puts analytical load on somebody else's OLTP
                                     database, at a time you chose. The first person to find out is on call
